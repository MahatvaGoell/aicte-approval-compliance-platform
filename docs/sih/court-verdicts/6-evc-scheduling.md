**The case:** Build smart EVC scheduling with conflict-of-interest checks as a headline feature of ApprovalTrack for the SIH 2026 national round.

**The charge:** The case fails as stated: it should not go ahead the way it is described.

## How the jury voted

| Juror | Vote | Why |
|---|---|---|

| The Statistician | GUILTY | There is no evidence in the record that this is where files stall, and nothing on how often a booking-constraint demo wins anything, so headlining it means betting the national round on a sample of zero, with six modules still unbuilt and the time left unknown. |
| The Teenager | GUILTY | Honestly the big finish is a booking getting a "no" error, which every app ever does, so nobody's gonna remember it next week, let alone call it extraordinary. |

## Charges that stuck

1. The headline is a routine booking error, not a finish. Six jurors called the rejected double booking ordinary or weak.
2. The conflict check runs on invented data with no cited AICTE rule. Five jurors leaned on this.
3. It answers a side question, not slipping deadlines and missing owners. Three jurors cited this.

## What the defense got right

1. The EVC stage is a real gap in the file's life, and the team has already promised the module. The prosecution concedes EVC "is a real step."
2. Suggest-then-confirm keeps a human in charge, as the team's own rules require. The prosecution concedes this point.

## Conditions for acquittal

1. Replace the double-booking climax with a named expert blocked from visiting their own institution, with the rule shown on screen. Four jurors asked for this.
2. Cite the AICTE handbook clause that defines EVC conflicts, or label the rule a demo assumption on screen. Five jurors asked for this.
3. Tie the blocked visit to the existing deadline and ownership view, and back the claim that EVC delays files with any real evidence the team can find. Jurors 1 and 4 asked for this.

## The sentence

Do not drop scheduling, but demote it from headline to supporting work. This week, swap the climax, cite the clause or label the assumption, and tie the visit to the deadline view. The record does not say how much time you have, so build only what you can finish. Do not claim real affiliation data until you have a source for it.
