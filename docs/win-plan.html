Start here

The honest read on this problem statement

No plan can promise a win. What a plan can do is remove every avoidable reason to lose and give the jury three or four moments they remember. That is what this document is for.

Four facts shape everything below:

  1. The jury knows this process better than you. AICTE runs SIH and wrote this problem statement. Its evaluators handle scrutiny, EVC and SAC files for a living. A team that uses the right words (EoA, LoA, deficiency, re-scrutiny, self-disclosure, Regional Office, Standing Appellate Committee) and models the real edge cases wins trust in the first minute. A team that shows a generic "application tracker" loses it.
  2. Most competing teams will build the same thing: a login, a form, a table of applications and a few charts. You beat them by making the time-based automation visible: clocks that run, pause, breach and escalate on their own, live in front of the jury.
  3. The evaluation criteria are written out for you: workflow accuracy, timeliness of alerts, reliability of deadline tracking, visibility of pending cases, reduction in manual follow-up and completeness of the audit trail. Every feature and every demo minute should map to one of these six (see the scoring map).
  4. The regulatory safeguard is a test. The statement says automation "shall not replace statutory scrutiny, verification, inspections or decisions". Build human sign-off into every decision step and say it out loud. Teams that pitch "AI approves applications" will be marked down.
On the motivation

Whatever the personal reason for wanting this, the jury only sees the product and the pitch. Put that energy into rehearsals and testing, which is where finals are actually decided.

What we are building

Product thesis: a rules engine with a dashboard on top

The core of ANUPALAN is a configurable engine that knows the APH. Dashboards are views of what the engine already knows. Five pillars:

1 · Route

Each application type follows a stage path defined in versioned configuration. Changing the APH means editing config, with no code change and no effect on files already in flight.

2 · Clock

Every stage, EVC, undertaking and document has a deadline clock. Clocks pause when a file is returned to the institution and resume on response, on a working-day calendar with holidays.

3 · Escalate

Configurable ladders: remind the assignee, then the supervisor, then the Regional Office, then the Bureau. Digests instead of spam.

4 · Watch

Undertakings and documents (lease deeds and others) are tracked as obligations with evidence, verification and expiry. OCR proposes the expiry date and an official confirms it.

5 · Prove

Every action, alert, submission and decision is written to a tamper-evident, hash-chained audit log, with the reason recorded for every return or revert.

Rule zero

The system recommends and reminds. A named official decides. Every decision screen shows who decides and records their action.

Week 1, non-negotiable

Domain homework that most teams skip

This is the cheapest advantage available. Assign one person full time to it for the first week.

  1. Read the APH. Download the Approval Process Handbook 2024-27 from aicte.gov.in plus the 2026 amendments and corrigenda. Extract: application categories (new institution, extension of approval, increase in intake, new course, change of name or site, closure, ODL/online), which ones need an EVC, prescribed timelines, land and lease requirements, and the conditions under which approval is granted with undertakings.
  2. Note the 2026 changes. The February 2026 corrigendum says EVCs are constituted by the Council (not the Regional Officer) and can be physical, online or hybrid, and that the Council constitutes the SAC, which hears appeals. Model EVC mode as a field and constitute committees at Council level in your role design. Mentioning this in the pitch signals you read the current rules.
  3. Interview your own college. Your institution files an AICTE application every year. Spend an hour with the person who does it (principal's office or AICTE coordinator). Ask: what gets stuck, what deficiencies come back, how they learn about deadlines, how lease or other document renewals are tracked today. Record quotes; one real quote in the deck is worth five charts.
  4. Collect real artefacts. Colleges publish EoA letters and SAC/SHC committee reports on their websites (search for "SAC_SHC" PDFs and "EOA report 2026-27"). Use their structure and field names for your forms and generated letters, so your screens look familiar to the jury.
  5. Get real scale numbers. Use AICTE's public dashboard for the number of approved institutions and their split by region and state. Size your synthetic data to match.
  6. Write a one-page glossary and a stage matrix (application type × stages × who acts × timeline). This becomes the seed for the workflow config and a backup slide.

Every number in the product or deck that you could not source gets labelled as configurable or illustrative. Never present an invented SLA as "the APH timeline".

Memorable moments

The eight differentiators and why judges care

Time machine most important

All code reads time from one Clock service. In demo mode the presenter jumps forward 7, 30 or 90 days and alerts, breaches and escalations fire live.

You cannot show "timeliness of alerts" in a 7-minute slot without it.

Pausable SLA clocks

Clock stops when a file goes back to the institution for a deficiency and restarts on response. Working days and a holiday calendar.

This is how officials actually think about pendency. It separates you from teams that just subtract dates.

Workflow Studio

A flowchart editor (React Flow) over the JSON config. Change an SLA from 15 to 10 days and the dashboard recomputes.

Answers "configurable rules aligned with the APH" by showing it.

Tamper-evident audit

Each audit event stores the hash of the previous one. Edit a row directly in the database during the demo and the integrity check shows exactly where the chain broke.

Direct proof for "completeness of the digital audit trail".

Lease OCR with human confirm

Upload a lease deed scan; the system highlights the dates it found and asks the official to confirm the expiry.

Removes data entry while keeping a human in the loop, exactly as the safeguard requires.

EVC panel and trip planner

Constraint solver (Google OR-Tools) assigns experts under conflict-of-interest, availability and load rules, and groups institutions in the same district into one visit trip.

A real operational saving officials will recognise.

Breach risk and bottlenecks

Simple, explainable forecasting: "Likely to breach in 4 days: Scrutiny queue in this region is running 2.1× slower than its 60-day median."

Moves dashboards from reporting to prevention. Keep it explainable; no black boxes.

Bilingual, accessible, sovereign

Hindi and English templates and UI, GIGW-minded accessibility, open-source stack, self-hosted LLM so no file leaves government servers.

Deployability is a scoring factor for a government jury.

Example escalation ladder (configurable)

T − 7 days

In-app reminder to the assigned officer or committee.

T − 2 days

Reminder by email and SMS; file turns amber on all dashboards.

T + 0

Breach. Officer and immediate supervisor alerted; file turns red; counted in the overdue report.

T + 3 days

Escalated to the Regional Office head with the case summary attached.

T + 7 days

Escalated to the Approval Bureau head; listed in the Chairman's weekly digest.

Documents use a parallel ladder: alerts to the institution at 90, 60, 30 and 7 days before expiry; on expiry the Regional Office is alerted; if renewal is still missing after the grace period, the file is flagged for the reviewer on the institution's next application. The flag is information for a human; it does not block anything by itself.

Engineering rules that make this reliable

  • One Clock service. Nothing calls now() directly. This is what makes the time machine and the tests possible.
  • Deadlines are rows, not cron guesses. A deadlines table holds every due date; a scheduler sweeps it every minute and fires each alert exactly once (idempotency key per alert).
  • Transition + audit in one database transaction. A stage move without its audit event cannot exist.
  • Audit table is append-only. Revoke UPDATE and DELETE at the database level; store hash = SHA-256(prev_hash + canonical JSON of event).
  • Scenario tests for every path. Write the expected stage sequence and alert timings for each route and assert them. "All 120 workflow scenarios pass" is a line for the pitch.

Seven minutes

Demo script: one file's life, then the big picture

Tell it as a story around one institution and one Regional Officer. Prepare a 3-minute, 5-minute and 8-minute cut, because jury slots vary.

0:00–0:40

The problem in one breath

Show the overdue count and the region heatmap on the Chairman dashboard.

"Today, follow-up on pendency, EVCs, undertakings and lease renewals happens over phone and email. ANUPALAN makes the rules run themselves and keeps a person in charge of every decision."

0:40–1:40

Submission and routing

An institution submits a new-institution application. The engine picks the Scrutiny + EVC path from the config and assigns it. Show the stage path on the file.

1:40–2:40

Deficiency and paused clock

Scrutiny officer returns the file with a reason and a deficiency list (auto-drafted letter, officer edits and sends). The SLA clock shows "paused". Institution responds; re-scrutiny starts and the clock resumes.

2:40–3:50

EVC queue and escalation

File moves to the EVC queue automatically. Solver proposes a panel with conflict checks and groups two nearby institutions into one trip. Presenter jumps the clock 12 days: EVC overdue, Regional Office escalation fires, an SMS lands on a teammate's phone.

3:50–5:00

Conditional approval, undertakings and lease

Competent authority approves with an undertaking. Institution uploads a lease deed scan; OCR proposes the expiry, official confirms. Jump 90 days: 60/30/7-day alerts, then expiry and escalation. Institution uploads the renewal; official verifies; case closes.

5:00–6:10

The big picture

Heatmap drill-down to a bottleneck, breach-risk list, workload rebalance, one-click region report. Workflow Studio: change one SLA and watch the dashboard recompute.

6:10–7:00

Proof and safeguard

Open the file's audit timeline. Edit one audit row in the database; "Verify integrity" pinpoints it.

"Every alert and recommendation supports the official. No approval, rejection or inspection outcome is ever taken by the system."

Deck

Deck and pitch structure

The SIH idea template is six slides. Keep the same spine for the finale deck and add a backup appendix.

  1. Title: PS ID 26262, title, theme, team. One line: "Rules that run themselves, decisions that stay human."
  2. Proposed solution: the five pillars as one diagram, one real quote from your college interview, and a screenshot of the working dashboard. A real screenshot at idea stage is a strong signal.
  3. Technical approach: architecture diagram, stack, the routing flowchart, the hash-chain idea in one picture.
  4. Feasibility and viability: open-source and self-hosted, runs on government cloud, adapters for existing AICTE systems, risks and their mitigations, the regulatory safeguard.

Appendix slides to pull up during Q&A: data model, security model, scaling numbers, integration plan, cost of running, roadmap after SIH.

Delivery rules

  • Demo beats slides. Spend at most 90 seconds on slides before the product is on screen.
  • One presenter drives the story, one drives the laptop. Everyone else answers questions in their area.
  • Use the jury's vocabulary. Say "file", "deficiency", "competent authority", "Regional Office".
  • When a judge suggests something, write it down visibly and, at the finale, build it before the next round. Judges remember being listened to.

Practice

Judge Q&A bank

How would this work with the existing AICTE portal?As a layer on top. An adapter reads application events from the portal (API, or a read-only replica / change-data-capture), and our engine adds routing, clocks and alerts. The mock portal API in the demo shows the contract.
What happens when the APH changes next year?Paths, SLAs and ladders are versioned configuration. New files use the new version; files in flight finish on the version they started with. Shown in the Workflow Studio.
Can the system approve or reject an application?No. Decision steps require a named competent authority's action. The system drafts, reminds and summarises; every draft is marked for review.
How do you avoid alert fatigue?Ladders start with the assignee only, then widen. Officials get digests instead of one message per file, and can tune channels per rule.
What if the OCR reads the wrong date?OCR only proposes. An official confirms the date, the confirmation is audited, and the original scan is always one click away.
Is data safe? Where does the LLM run?Self-hosted model, no external API. Role and region scoping on every query, encrypted document storage, virus scanning, OTP login, full audit. Aligned with the DPDP Act 2023 and CERT-In practice.
Will it scale to every institution and every cycle?Peak load is a few thousand transitions a day, well within one Postgres instance. Stateless API scales horizontally; give real numbers from your load test.
An officer goes on leave. What happens to their files?Delegation: the supervisor reassigns in bulk or a standing delegate is set; clocks keep running and the audit records the handover.
How did you validate the workflow?Against the APH text, published SAC/SHC and EoA documents, an interview with our own college's AICTE coordinator, and a scenario test suite for every path.
How is the audit trail tamper-proof?Append-only table with UPDATE/DELETE revoked, plus a hash chain. Demonstrate the broken chain. Mention that the daily chain head can be anchored externally (for example, signed and archived).
What would it cost to run?Open-source stack on existing government cloud; a handful of VMs. No licence fees.
Why not just use an off-the-shelf BPM tool?A generic BPM can route, but AICTE needs APH-specific clocks that pause on deficiency, undertaking and document lifecycles, and region-scoped dashboards. We kept the engine small and the rules in config.
